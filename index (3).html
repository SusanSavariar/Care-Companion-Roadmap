Care Companion V1 roadmap

105 features sorted by proposed stage and ranked by priority score within each column. Next and Later items live in one Backlog, marked with a Next or Later tag, and Out items sit at the very bottom of it with an Out tag. Drag a card to another column, or use its Move menu, to promote it into V1 or park it. Drag V1 tiles into the week columns to the right to schedule them.

Stages come from the Proposed Stage column. Proposed Next, Later and Out items start in the Backlog with a matching tag; Next sorts first and Out sorts last. Three features have no proposal yet and start in Needs triage. Score is the priority score from the scope sheet; unscored items sort last. Your schedule, new tiles and week settings are saved in this browser only, so use Copy sprint plan as CSV to take them elsewhere.
